Mainnet startup in src/server.js now requires distinct HOUSE_ADDRESS and FEE_ADDRESS, a validated mainnet XRPL connection, KYC file, configured Xaman credentials, and a fee-wallet Regular Key authorized by the validated FEE_ADDRESS account root. It creates mainnet Xaman signing payloads for borrower collateral, lender fee, borrower CheckCash, CheckCancel, and house shortfall transactions. Participant accounts sign and fund their own transactions; no mainnet faucet funding is implemented.

Loan sessions and transaction hashes are written to an atomic local JSON journal, restored at startup, and protected by a single-process lock. Pending collateral sessions are not silently deleted on timeout. The systemd unit stores the journal under /var/lib/h-kyc.

The HTTP service now caps concurrent connections, request duration, header size/count, per-socket request count, tracked client fingerprints, and loan requests per rolling day. Repeated invalid-proof tracking expires and is bounded. These controls are implemented in src/server.js and src/guard.js.

The external security audit has not been completed. The review team should independently exercise the mainnet paths, durable journal interruption/restart cases, Xaman payload resolution and rejection, fee Regular Key authorization, Check replay rejection, settlement payment exactness, resource limits, and error paths. No live mainnet transactions were sent during implementation.

The Testnet demo saves faucet wallet signing seeds in poc/.fifo-demo-v2.json. Protect this file and do not copy it to a public or production machine. Demo default sale proceeds are issuer-funded simulations, not a real DEX sale.

The disclaimer text is a placeholder for the legal team's announcement. The tests only verify program behavior and do not assess legal terms.
